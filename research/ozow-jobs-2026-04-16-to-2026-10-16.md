# Ozow public job-opening record: 16 April–16 October 2026

Research cut-off: **16 October 2026 (Africa/Johannesburg)**. This is an evidence ledger of **27 distinct role records** found in public sources, not an estimate of hires or net headcount. The companion [CSV](ozow-jobs-2026-04-16-to-2026-10-16.csv) has one row per record and machine-readable URLs.

## How to read the dates

- **Reported posting date** is the date given by Greenhouse's `first_published` field or by a named job board. A syndicator's date can be an ingestion or repost date. Dates before 16 April are retained where an in-window source showed the listing open.
- **First/last observed** are the earliest and latest *dated evidence recovered within this window*. A one-day interval means only one dated observation was found. It is not an opening-to-closing interval, and an archived listing alone does not prove it remained open later.
- **Open evidence** means a dated Wayback capture with an application action and no closed banner, a dated board-monitor observation, or the employer's current Greenhouse API. A page that already said “no longer accepting applications” is not treated as open.
- **Likely team** follows Ozow's Greenhouse department when available; otherwise it is inferred from the job description and labelled as such. Titles are copied from the source; spelling variants and reposts are recorded in the same row when the underlying listing is identifiable.

Sources checked: [Ozow careers](https://ozow.com/jobs), [live Greenhouse board](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/ozow), public [Greenhouse job API](https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/ozow/jobs?content=true), [Greycroft's Ozow board](https://jobs.greycroft.com/companies/ozow), dated Wayback captures linked below, [LinkedIn jobs and Ozow posts](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ozowsecurepayments/jobs/), plus JobsRadar, InterviewOS, ApplyAll, Indeed and other job-board records where useful. No paid service, account sign-in or API key was used.

## Operational questions worth investigating

These are **hypotheses for discovery**, not claims about Ozow's current operations. Each comes from advertised responsibilities. Validate with the relevant owner before proposing a change.

1. **Merchant onboarding to first transaction.** R11 explicitly owns time to first transaction, handoffs and merchant follow-up; R12 owns lifecycle engagement; R18 reviews KYC; R14 supports integrations. Hypothesis: a shared funnel view and clearer ownership at KYC, integration, activation and Customer Success boundaries could reduce time spent locating stalled merchants. Investigate stage timestamps, reason codes, handoff queues and exceptions before designing automation.
2. **Portfolio and launch coordination.** R03 calls for portfolio visibility, dependency tracking, launch briefs and decision logs; R01 calls for cross-functional delivery and risk tracking; R10 calls for roadmap trade-off visibility. Hypothesis: teams may benefit from one source of status and a small launch-readiness checklist. Compare actual planning artifacts, status-reconciliation effort and launch incidents rather than assuming duplication.
3. **Recurring support and integration issues.** R06 must log multi-channel payment queries and feed recurring pain points back; R14 diagnoses merchant API integrations and works to SLAs. Hypothesis: linking ticket categories, API error patterns, docs gaps and engineering fixes could lower repeat contacts. Review anonymised ticket themes, escalation loops and time to resolution.
4. **Billing, commissions and reconciliation.** R04 describes merchant invoicing, credit notes, distributor commissions, supplier reconciliation and bank allocations; R05 describes access matrices, debtor follow-up and document control; R13 describes data-quality pipelines. Hypothesis: a traceable data-to-invoice and payment reconciliation workflow could reduce manual exception handling. Measure exception frequency, root causes, approval waits and audit-evidence retrieval.
5. **Fraud, KYC and security evidence flow.** R18 asks for defensible KYC case trails; R21 for fraud rules, alert capacity and investigations; R22 for vulnerability remediation and audit evidence; R10 for trust-and-risk tooling. Hypothesis: common case identifiers, clearer control ownership and automated evidence capture might reduce rework across functions. First map case volumes, false positives, handoffs and control SLAs.
6. **Marketing production flow.** R23 owns brief intake, priorities, agencies and studio capacity; R24 turns briefs into content and tracks production. Hypothesis: a visible brief-to-publish queue could expose bottlenecks and clarify review responsibility. Sample recent briefs and measure wait time at each approval step.
7. **Engineering delivery and AI safeguards.** R07 calls for CI/CD and infrastructure automation; R25–R27 call for AI evaluations, controlled integrations and disciplined AI coding. Hypothesis: targeted automation could remove toil if paired with release checks and human approval. Start with deployment lead time, rollback and failure data, then test one bounded use case.

## Public-record gaps

- Wayback captures of the employer and Greycroft boards are sparse. A role could have opened and closed between captures, especially before systematic third-party tracking began in June.
- Greenhouse's public API lists only current jobs. Removed postings often return 404, and its `first_published` field can refer to an older posting instance while syndicators show a later repost.
- LinkedIn public search is incomplete and sometimes shows relative ages or recruiter reposts; a sign-in may expose more history, but no authenticated browser was needed for the confirmed records here.
- Job aggregators may retain closed pages, rename titles, import stale copies or assign their own dates. Their evidence is labelled rather than used to infer continuous availability.
- Vacancies, requisitions and actual hires are different measures. The record cannot prove team headcount growth, the number of seats per title, exact closing dates or that a listed responsibility reflects a current operational problem.
